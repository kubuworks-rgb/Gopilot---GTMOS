# MVP Scope

## Included

- Demo authentication and tenant-scoped workspace.
- Product profile, fixture research workflow, findings, exactly three ICPs.
- Evidence/source model, account discovery, signals, deterministic scoring.
- Opportunity brief, evidence drawer, campaign draft approval/edit/rejection.
- Formula-safe CSV export and audit events.
- Safe research gateway contracts, SSRF validation, Agent Reach health wrapper.
- PostgreSQL/Redis production shape and fixture-mode local execution.

## Deferred

Live paid search providers, contact enrichment, CRM/email/calendar connectors,
scheduled monitoring, enterprise SSO/billing, Temporal, autonomous outreach, and all
cookie-backed social research. Interfaces are documented but these capabilities are
not represented as working integrations.
