# Product Specification

## Promise

Turn a founder-led B2B company's product and target into ranked account
opportunities that explain why the account fits, why now, the evidence supporting
each material claim, and the best next human-reviewed GTM action.

## User journey

Workspace and product profile -> research plan and run -> evidence-backed market
findings -> exactly three ICP candidates -> selected ICP -> account ranking ->
account opportunity brief -> campaign draft -> approve/edit/reject -> safe CSV export.

## Primary object

`AccountOpportunityBrief` contains account identity, deterministic score breakdowns,
evidence-linked fit/intent claims, risks, recommended problem/offer/action, and a
human-reviewed campaign draft. The product optimizes for accepted opportunities,
not scraped row counts.
